# Announcement — design prompt + X post

Two deliverables for the ladder update going live. Section 1 is the prompt to
paste into Claude Design. Section 2 is the X copy, ready to post.

---

## 1. Design prompt (paste into Claude Design)

Design an in-app announcement graphic for **Blokaz**, a block-puzzle mini app on
Celo that runs inside MiniPay. The message: **a big update to the level ladder is
live**.

### Brand — follow this exactly, it is an existing product

Neo-brutalist. Thick black borders, hard offset drop shadows (no blur), flat
fills, slight rotations on badges and chips, zero gradients, zero soft edges.

```
Paper (background)   #f5efe3        Ink (borders, text)  #0c0c10
Yellow               #ffd51f        Pink                 #ff3bbd
Lime                 #b7ff3b        Cyan                 #29e6e6
Orange               #ff7a1a        Purple               #8a3dff

Display type   Archivo Black — uppercase, tight tracking (-0.03em), very large
Body type      Space Grotesk — 600 weight, generous line height
Borders        3–4px solid ink        Shadows  4–6px offset, solid ink, no blur
```

Everything sits on paper. Accent colours are used as **flat fills behind black
text**, one accent per element — never as gradients, never more than three
accents in one composition.

### Sizes

1. **1080×1080** square — the in-app news card and Instagram.
2. **1600×900** — the X post image.
3. **1080×1920** — story format, optional.

### Content hierarchy

**Eyebrow chip** (small, rotated ~-2°, yellow fill, black text):
`UPDATE · LIVE NOW`

**Headline** (largest element, 3–4 words per line, black on paper):
`EVERY LEVEL NOW STARTS YOU FROM ZERO`

**Sub-line** (Space Grotesk, one sentence):
`The ladder was rebuilt. Each rung is its own week's work — nothing carries over.`

**Four feature blocks**, each a bordered box with its own accent fill, a short
all-caps label and one line of body copy:

| Accent | Label | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Pink | FRESH START EVERY LEVEL | Clear a level and all four objectives reset. No inherited progress. |
| Lime | CASH REWARDS, ON THE LEVEL | 18 this season — 10 at level 4, 5 at level 8, 3 at level 12. Claim yours on the level that paid it. |
| Cyan | DAILY STREAK IS LIVE | Play once a day, keep the run alive. It follows your wallet, not your phone. |
| Purple | CAREER LEVELS NEVER END | The cap is gone. There is always a next level. |

**Footer strip** (ink fill, yellow text):
`blokaz.xyz · @playblokaz`

### Motifs to use

Blokaz is a block puzzle — use **tetromino-style blocks** as decoration: a few
scattered squares and L-shapes in accent colours with black borders and hard
shadows, tumbling in from a corner or filling a strip. Do not draw a board.

The ladder is a real 12-rung progression with badges at each rung. A **small
stepped-bar motif** (four or five ascending blocks, the tallest in yellow) reads
as "levels" instantly and is worth including near the headline.

### Rules

- No stock photography, no 3D renders, no glow, no gradient meshes.
- Keep the headline readable at thumbnail size — it must survive being 200px wide
  in a feed.
- No numbers that could date the graphic (no "v2.1", no dates).
- Reward **counts** are on the graphic (10 / 5 / 3) — they are the size of the
  season's offer and the scarcity is the point. Reward **amounts** are not,
  unless asked: the in-app UI says "PLUS A CASH REWARD" without figures, and the
  graphic should not promise more specifically than the product does.
